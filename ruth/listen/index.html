Ruth (Audio)

Listen offline · Regeneration Calvary Chapel

Take the whole Book of Ruth with you: on a trip, at work, anywhere with no signal. Add it once to a podcast app on your phone, and every message downloads so you can listen offline.

1. Tap your podcast app

🎙️Apple PodcastsiPhone and iPad (already on your phone) ▶︎Pocket CastsiPhone and Android ◉OvercastiPhone Copied. Open your podcast app and paste it.

2. Download for offline

  1. Tap Follow (or Subscribe) in the app.
  2. Tap the download arrow ⬇︎ next to a message, or turn on automatic downloads for the show.
  3. Downloaded messages play with no Internet or cell service.

New messages in this series show up in the app by themselves.
